# Research Report: Find 5 Indian B2B SaaS startups in HR tech and summarize their positioning

**Status:** `OK` | **Confidence:** `HIGH` (82.0%)

## Short Answer
Five prominent Indian B2B SaaS startups in the HR tech space are **Darwinbox**, **Keka**, **greytHR**, **inFeedo (Amber)**, and **Leena AI**. Their market positioning spans full-lifecycle enterprise Human Capital Management (Darwinbox), user-centric mid-market payroll & automation (Keka, greytHR), and AI-driven conversational employee engagement and helpdesk automation (inFeedo, Leena AI).

## Key Findings
1. **Darwinbox** positions itself as a mobile-first, enterprise-grade HCM platform for large enterprises across Asia, competing directly with SAP SuccessFactors and Workday with comprehensive hire-to-retire modules. (Sources: `[s1]`)
2. **Keka** focuses on mid-sized enterprises, positioning around high-usability employee experience, automated payroll processing, and attendance tracking with minimal implementation friction. (Sources: `[s2]`)
3. **greytHR** caters primarily to Indian SMBs with a focus on compliant statutory payroll calculations (PF, ESI, TDS) and cloud-based core HR administration. (Sources: `[s3]`)
4. **inFeedo** positions its flagship AI bot 'Amber' as a predictive employee retention and sentiment analytics platform that proactively identifies attrition risks through continuous conversational check-ins. (Sources: `[s4]`)
5. **Leena AI** positions itself as an autonomous employee experience platform integrating generative AI and automated service desk resolution across HR, IT, and workplace workflows. (Sources: `[s5]`)
